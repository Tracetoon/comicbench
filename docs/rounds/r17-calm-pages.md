## What changed (against round 16's plain arm)

- **At most two action beats per page**, enforced by `comic check`; every other beat stays still, in calm, roomy panels. (In round 16 about half of all panels were action beats.)
- **Three-across structures** (a band of three panels) are drawn about a third as often. They had round 16's weakest visuals.
- **Layouts are assigned for 3 and 4 panels only.** Five-panel pages read cramped at PR width.
- **The author no longer sees species labels** for audited archetypes, and the contradicting core and variant wording is corrected (P06, P10, P30).
- **The brainstorm is shelved by default** for the built-in anime genre (`brainstorm: on` restores it).

## What we learned

1. **Calmer pages fixed most of the visual loss.**
   - Visual rose from 33% to 47% against r12.
   - Against round 16's plain arm, round 17 wins visual 57% and overall 59%.
   - Fewer slants, fewer three-across bands and no five-panel pages read better at PR width.
2. **The flow is now level with round 12 overall** (46%, nearly half the pairs tied) and better on the craft that sets comics apart: funnier 61%, use of the medium 68%.
3. **Veteran appeal remains the weak spot** (36% against r12). The anime-fan layer of the jokes isn't landing; the shelved meme list could return here as an optional list for the single author.
4. **The page-filled question** should exclude the outer page margin, which is by design.
