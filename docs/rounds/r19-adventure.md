## What changed (against round 18)

- **Leads:** the reborn spider, the vending machine and the military prodigy are never cast; the military preset is retired.
- **Gutters:** every gutter is the same width in pixels, rows and columns alike.
- **Adventure settings:** the host assigns one of 12 adventure settings per comic (each rests 3 comics). The check rejects office-like places and office-parody lines, and the two office staples are no longer offered.
- **Staples:** staples whose topic matches the PR's text are offered 3× as often.
- **Payoff anchor:** an anchor term must be lettered in the last panel.
- **Layout sentence:**
  - counts first ("4 panels in 3 rows: 2, then 1, then 1");
  - relative band sizes;
  - no implied breakouts.

## What we learned

1. **No office parody, and a page that works much better as a comic.**
   - The desk, counter and paperwork jokes are gone.
   - Use of the medium jumped to the best of any round (93% against real comics; r18: 80%): settings worth drawing, with action in them.
2. **The PR got harder to see.**
   - "About this PR" fell clearly: below r12 with CIs apart, and below r18.
   - The judge's reasons show why. In r18 the desk and screen scenes let a comic letter the PR's specifics ("LearnTuiSession", "Showing 40 lines • e to expand", "four-part PE version").
   - In r19 the same change plays out as an adventure metaphor (two campfires for a split release job), and the specifics drop off the page.
   - Removing the office removed the easy surface for technical lettering.
3. **Humor and overall dipped slightly but within noise.** Funnier 18% vs r18's 26%; overall 8% vs 12%. CIs overlap, and the lower "about this PR" likely drags overall.
4. **Staple use stays low** (6/40) even with PR matching. Offering alone does not get staples used.

## Next (round 20)

- **Keep:** the settings, the office check, the retired leads, uniform gutters.
- **Put the PR back on the page, in-world.** An adventure setting still has surfaces:
  - a status window;
  - a quest notice, a signpost, a rune tablet;
  - a summoning-circle readout.
  Require two anchor terms lettered on such a surface, one of them in the payoff, and name that device in the packet ("show the change's real names on an in-world surface: a status window, sign or notice").
- **Staples:** to raise use, consider making the PR-matched staple the default unless the author names a better fit, rather than one optional choice of three.
