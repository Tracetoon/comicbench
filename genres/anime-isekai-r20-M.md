Each character is a story role plus a visible silhouette plus a capability plus a behavioral contradiction; the joke is the contradiction inside genuine competence. Species is shared visual vocabulary, never a personality; nobody is only an admirer of the hero; romance stays off. Beastfolk and other peoples are companions, never servants or captives. Outfits stay practical, with no fan service. A time loop is a reset, never a death.

How the joke works (round 20): the last line only works because of what this PR changes: it reinterprets the change itself, and it would mean nothing with the PR removed. Never end on a character's unrelated quirk (food, money, vanity, a pet), a pun on the setting, or slapstick for its own sake. Build one setup and repeat it with one thing varied, so the turn is a contrast, then land the payoff in one short plain line of at most 8 words. A second laugh is welcome only if it reuses a word or object already on the page.

Choose the material first (round 20): before any setting or cast, list three details of this PR that could carry a joke, and pick the one that is both central to the PR's purpose and closest to a frustration every developer already knows (naming chaos, a smoke test that smokes, a missing dependency, a flaky check).
